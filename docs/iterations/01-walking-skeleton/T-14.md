# T-14: Profile setup (S-02) and Settings: profile (S-21) screens

**Tier:** B (UI from approved mockups over finished Actions and Selectors)
**Links:** FR-002 AC3 (the card must be on setup); FR-003 AC2, AC3, AC4 (first half); FR-023 (field);
FR-004 AC3 (groundwork); NFR-007, NFR-041; mockups **S-02** (Normal, Error), **S-21** (Business profile

## Why
These are the two screens the owner will touch in the demo: setup right after sign-up, and the profile
settings afterwards. They also carry the first end-to-end test of the whole sign-up path on a phone.

## What to build (decisions already made)
**S-02 `/w/<id>/setup/profile`** ("Step 1 of 2 · about 2 minutes", "Just what your first invoice
needs"), following the mockup field order and copy:
- Legal name (as on PAN); Business type (Individual / Sole proprietor, "Companies and LLPs are not
  supported yet."); PAN with hint "10 characters, like ABCDE1234F."; State (full list from the rules
  package, "Decides which GST threshold applies to you."); Bank account for invoices (holder name,
  account number, IFSC); collapsed "Optional: GSTIN, trading name, handles"; "Income already earned this
  tax year (<current tax year>)" opening total in rupees (stored as paise; accepts Indian commas).
- **Second sign-in method card** (PRD M1; not in the mockup): placed directly above the Save button,
  titled "Add a second way to sign in", text "So nobody can get in with your phone number alone. We
  will email you a code.", using T-11's `_second_method_card.html`. When verified it shows a green
  "Verified" chip and the address.
- **Save and continue:** runs `profile.save_basic`, then `profile.add_pan` / `add_gstin` /
  `add_payout_bank` for fields entered for the first time, all in one request; any validation error
  re-renders the form with inline errors (`role="alert"`, field linked by `aria-describedby`) and saves
  nothing (one transaction around the Actions). If the second method is not verified yet, the page
  shows "Verify your email to continue" next to the card and stays.
- **"Skip for now, I will add it when I invoice":** skips the profile fields but still requires the
  second method (M1); then continues.
- Continue goes to Home (`/w/<id>/`) in this iteration (S-03 "Connect sources" is step 2 later; the
  step label still reads "Step 1 of 2").
- A sensitive field already saved (e.g. the creator comes back) shows masked and read-only here.

**S-21 `/w/<id>/settings/profile`** (owner only; non-owners get 404 because the screen is owner-only in
PRD section 6):
- "Business profile" card: legal name, trading name, state with "GST threshold used: Rs X" (from
  `profile_view`, Indian digit grouping), handles; Save runs `profile.save_basic` and shows a "Saved"
  toast (`role="status"`). The "Invoice number prefix" row from the mockup is omitted until invoices
  exist (iteration 4).
- "Sensitive details" card with the mockup's lead text. PAN, GSTIN, bank shown masked. For a field
  never saved: an "Add" button opening an inline form that runs the first-entry Action (allowed without
  a second check, PRD M2). For a saved field: "Change" is a disabled button with the text "Needs a
  second check: coming soon" (iteration 2). The "Last change" line shows the latest sensitive log entry
  summary if any.
- The "Devices and sessions" card is not shown in this iteration (iteration 2).
- Loading uses skeleton rows only for HTMX partial refreshes; Error state per the mockup ("Your change
  was not saved... Try again") when an Action fails unexpectedly.

**Money input:** a small helper parses "6,00,000", "600000", "6,00,000.50" into paise, rejects
fractions of a paisa and negatives, and is unit-tested.

## Acceptance criteria
- **FR-003 AC2 (verbatim):** Given a PAN in the wrong format, when saved, then it is refused with a plain
  message. (On S-02: the mockup's error text shows under the field and nothing is saved.)
- **FR-002 AC3 (verbatim):** Given a creator who signed up with a phone code, when profile setup
  finishes, then a verified email address or a linked Google account is on the account.
- Given a new creator who fills name, PAN, state, business type and bank and leaves GSTIN empty, when
  they tap Save and continue (with email verified), then they land on Home and the log shows the
  profile, PAN and bank entries, with no second check asked (FR-003 AC4 first half).
- Given Mizoram saved, when S-21 opens, then it shows "GST threshold used: Rs 10,00,000" (FR-003 AC3).
- Given saved PAN and bank, when S-21 opens, then they show only masked, and "Change" is disabled.
- Given GSTIN never saved, when the owner taps Add on S-21 and enters a valid GSTIN, then it is saved
  and logged without a second check.
- Given the full sign-up path on a phone viewport, when run end to end, then it completes and axe finds
  no serious or critical violations on S-01, S-02, Home, activity log and S-21 (NFR-007, NFR-041).
