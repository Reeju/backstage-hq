them. Q-H1 costs a few cents of hourly-billed resources and an afternoon; finding out after T-15 would
mean rebuilding staging.

  (NFR-006); proposed edit to ADR-015 and section 26 S7. Needed before iteration 5.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given each of the three spikes, when finished, then `spike-results.md` has a yes/no answer, dated
  evidence with links, what it decides, and (if "no") the fallback and proposed document edit.
- Given Q-H1, when finished, then it records the database settings and the `CLIENT_IP_HEADER` value that
  T-15 and T-10 use, and the dump and restore steps for the 14a runbook.
- Given the spike scripts, when inspected, then they hold no credentials (read from a local file ignored
  by git), and test tokens are revoked at the end (recorded).
- Given the DigitalOcean and AWS resources created, when the spike ends, then they are deleted
  (recorded with the final cost).
