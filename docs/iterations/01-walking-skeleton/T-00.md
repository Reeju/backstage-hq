before it is made, because it touches approved documents.

**Depends on:** owner approval, and the owner's answer to plan section 11 decision 5 (how to label

committed." and section 29 and all 20 ADRs say "Proposed", but git shows the architecture was approved
in commit 1abed76 (and amended in 22a4736 and 51c6da6). The PRD had the same problem and fixed it (P5).
Builders read these labels; "Proposed" invites them to re-argue settled decisions.
