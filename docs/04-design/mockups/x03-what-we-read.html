Gmail

We read
Emails that look like brand work, invoices, payments and tax, from the last 12 months and new ones as they arrive (paid plan).
We keep
Business emails only, so every value can link to its source. Personal, fan, newsletter and spam: only the category, date and Gmail's message ID. Never the text.
We never
Delete, move, archive or send any email without your tap. We never take bank details from an email.
Disconnect
Settings, Connections, Disconnect. Choose to keep your records or delete everything from Gmail.

Instagram DMs

We read new DM threads on your professional account. We keep only brand enquiries. Fan and personal DMs are not stored. We never reply without your tap, or follow or message anyone.

WhatsApp forward-to number

We read only what you forward from your verified numbers, and keep only the business part. Anything from other numbers is ignored and not kept beyond 24 hours. We never read your personal chats. We only reply "Got it".

Forwarding address and uploads

Emails from your verified addresses are processed like connected email. Others are discarded after 7 days. Uploaded files are scanned for malware before anyone can open them.
