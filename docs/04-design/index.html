BackstageHQ: MVP mockups

Clickable, static, sample data (creator "Arjun Mehta", Pune; today is Monday 9 November 2026). Built from the approved PRD (screens S-01 to S-30) and architecture. Nothing here sends anything or saves anything.

How to click through

  • Open any screen. It is phone-first; widen the window for the desktop layout (side menu).
  • The dark bar at the top of each screen has buttons for its states: Normal, Loading, Empty, Error and others. Tap them to see each one.
  • Violet always means AI. AI suggestions show a source link, can be edited, and do nothing until a person taps. Green padlock notes mark the send rule.
  • Gap Gn pink dashed notes mark a placeholder I chose where the PRD is silent. All are listed below.
  • Needs internet once for the Tailwind script (cdn.tailwindcss.com). Without it, pages still open with the shared stylesheet, but layout is plainer. Design rules: design-system.md.
